# Intraday Scalping Strategy

## Strategy Overview
This strategy is an intraday scalping strategy designed to generate small but consistent profits by trading short-term price movements.

The strategy focuses on volatility, liquidity, and short-term market microstructure rather than long-term market direction.

The goal of the strategy is to generate consistent intraday PnL while strictly controlling risk.

---

## Core Concept
The core idea of scalping is:

- Trade short-term price movements
- Cut losses very quickly
- Repeat small trades many times
- Avoid large overnight risk

In summary:

Small profit, small loss, many repetitions.

---

## When to Use the Strategy

The scalping strategy is mainly used when:

| Market Condition | Reason |
|------------------|-------|
| High volatility | More price movement |
| High liquidity | Easy execution |
| Intraday trends | Short-term momentum |
| News / Events | Increased movement |

Scalping works best when the market is moving actively intraday.

---

## Trade Structure

Typical scalping trade structure:

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Holding time | Seconds to minutes |
| Profit target | Small |
| Stop loss | Very small |
| Position size | Medium |
| Frequency | High |

The strategy relies on high trade frequency rather than large profits per trade.

---

## Risk Management

Risk management is the most important part of scalping.

| Risk | Control |
|------|---------|
| Large loss | Very tight stop loss |
| Overtrading | Limit number of trades |
| Slippage | Trade liquid markets |
| Emotional trading | Follow strict rules |

Key principle:

Cut losses immediately. Do not hold losing positions.

---

## PnL Structure

The strategy generates profit from:

1. Short-term price movement
2. Bid-ask spread
3. Volatility spikes
4. Market microstructure inefficiencies

PnL comes from many small trades rather than a few large trades.

---

## Strengths and Weaknesses

| Strength | Weakness |
|---------|----------|
| Works in volatile markets | Difficult in low volatility |
| Low overnight risk | Requires fast execution |
| Frequent opportunities | High transaction cost |
| Small drawdown | Mentally demanding |

---

## Summary

This strategy can be summarized as:

An intraday trading strategy that generates profits from short-term price movements while strictly controlling risk through tight stop-loss and disciplined execution.
